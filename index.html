About me

I finished my Master of Science in Mathematics in 2024 and completed my Master's Thesis under the supervision of Vera Traub.

I am part of the Graz School of Discrete Mathematics.

My research interests lie in graph algorithms, combinatorial optimization, and complexity. So far, my work has primarily focused on randomized algorithms in sparse graph classes.

Outside of work, I enjoy all kinds of sports. In summer, I spend as much time as possible surfing, while in winter, you can find snowboarding in the mountains or playing basketball. Apart from sports, I enjoy going to concerts and festivals, visiting museums, and spending evenings at the theater.
